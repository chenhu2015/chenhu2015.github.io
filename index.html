About

I work on LLM post-training at Amazon AWS. Most of my time goes into reinforcement learning for coding agents: the task data, the sandboxed environments the agent works in, the rewards and verifiers, multi-node GPU training, and the held-out evaluations that tell us whether a change actually helped.

Before this, I built math and code SFT data checked with formal verification (Lean) and ran SFT training for Amazon Nova, and worked on prompt optimization and hallucination detection for Alexa.

I have a Ph.D. in Civil Engineering, where I built statistical and physics-based models of material failure, and an M.S. in Computer Science, both from the University of Minnesota.
